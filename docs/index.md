# Samuel Kyama

### Senior Software Engineer :sunglasses:

![Image title](./assets/portrait-photo-samuel.png){ align=left }

I am a `Senior Software Engineer based in Kenya`. I build practical software systems for businesses, public-sector teams, fintech products, and AI-powered platforms. I enjoy turning real-world problems into reliable products that people can actually use, maintain, and grow.

My work spans backend engineering, frontend applications, APIs, payments, automation, deployment, and AI/ML product workflows. I have led and contributed to systems used by millions of people, including national-scale health platforms, payment integrations, business software, and AI-assisted applications.

Besides creating software, I love God. When I'm not staring at my laptop, I'm probably in a church somewhere. I also love music, so I will probably always have one ear warm at any given time.

### What I Build :tools:

I mostly work on products that sit at the intersection of `software engineering`, `business operations`, and `automation`.

- AI-powered platforms such as [LitCareers](https://www.litcareers.org/){ target="_blank" rel="noopener" }, where users upload CVs, get matched with relevant jobs, and receive AI-driven CV improvement suggestions.
- AI/ML-enabled media products such as [Piczangu](https://piczangu.com/){ target="_blank" rel="noopener" }, a photo storage platform with facial recognition based retrieval.
- Public health platforms such as [Chanjo Kenya](https://chanjo.health.go.ke/){ target="_blank" rel="noopener" } and the [Kenya Ministry of Health Vaccination Portal](https://portal.health.go.ke/){ target="_blank" rel="noopener" }, which supported Covid-19 vaccination records, certificates, reporting, and facility workflows.
- Fintech and payment systems, including M-Pesa integrations, paybill abstractions, payment APIs, and business tools for Kenyan companies.
- Infrastructure and automation products such as SFNetworks, a VPS provisioning platform built around Django REST, Next.js, Proxmox, and M-Pesa.
- Web platforms for organizations in education, insurance, church ministry, legal services, and enterprise operations.

### AI/ML Work :material-robot:

I am especially interested in AI that is useful inside real products, not just demos. My AI/ML work includes job matching, CV analysis, recommendation workflows, and facial recognition based search. I like building the full product around the model: the API, user experience, data flow, storage, permissions, deployment, and feedback loops that make the AI feature useful in everyday use.

Some examples:

- Built AI-powered job matching and CV improvement workflows for LitCareers.
- Built facial recognition based photo retrieval workflows for Piczangu.
- Built data-heavy public health systems where reporting, accuracy, auditability, and reliability mattered at national scale.

I encountered computers in a high school lab at Starehe Boys Centre. My passion was ignited when I discovered that I could write simple bash scripts to automate my workflow. Since then, I have never looked back.

#### BSc Computer Science, University of Nairobi :fontawesome-solid-graduation-cap:

I studied Computer Science at the University of Nairobi, where I built the technical foundation that shaped the way I think about software engineering, systems, and problem solving.

Most notably, I led engineering work on [Chanjo Kenya](https://chanjo.health.go.ke/){ target="_blank" rel="noopener" } and the [Kenya Ministry of Health Vaccination Portal](https://portal.health.go.ke/){ target="_blank" rel="noopener" }. These platforms supported Kenya's Covid-19 vaccination program, serving millions of Kenyans and enabling vaccination certificate access during a critical national moment.
